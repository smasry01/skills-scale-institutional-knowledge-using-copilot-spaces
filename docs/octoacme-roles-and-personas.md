# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Agile Coach helps the team work effectively, removes impediments, and improves execution quality through consistent agile practices. They focus on flow, collaboration, and delivery health rather than direct feature ownership.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Remove blockers and coordinate escalations for team dependencies
- Coach the team on agile practices, rituals, and continuous improvement
- Help keep work visible and aligned with sprint goals
- Support team health, focus, and delivery predictability

### Goals
- Improve team delivery flow and predictability
- Reduce friction in execution and communication
- Strengthen adoption of agile practices and continuous learning

### Typical Interactions
- With Developers: Helps unblock work, facilitates planning, and supports team process improvements
- With Product Managers: Aligns backlog priorities with delivery rhythm and sprint capacity
- With Project Managers: Coordinates timelines, risks, and escalation paths
- With Stakeholders: Shares delivery progress in a way that is clear and actionable

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA / Testing Lead owns the quality strategy for a project, ensuring features meet acceptance criteria and are ready for release. They help translate requirements into robust validation steps and catch issues before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and quality gates for the project
- Review acceptance criteria and identify testability gaps
- Coordinate manual QA, automated tests, and regression coverage
- Validate feature readiness before release
- Report quality risks, defects, and release readiness to stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Ensure a clear, consistent quality bar for features
- Support confident, timely releases

### Typical Interactions
- With Developers: Collaborates on test design, automation, and defect triage
- With Product Managers: Validates whether acceptance criteria are met
- With Project Managers: Shares release readiness and quality blockers
- With Stakeholders: Reports confidence in feature quality and release risk

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Architect provides technical direction and governance across the solution. They ensure the implementation aligns with platform standards, technical constraints, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architectural decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and platform trade-offs
- Guide the team on scalability, maintainability, and integration strategy
- Ensure solutions align with standards, security requirements, and performance goals
- Support large-scale design decisions and cross-team technical alignment

### Goals
- Improve technical quality and long-term maintainability
- Reduce architectural drift and implementation risk
- Enable confident delivery of complex solutions

### Typical Interactions
- With Developers: Reviews design decisions, architecture, and technical trade-offs
- With Product Managers: Advises on feasibility, sequencing, and technical constraints
- With Project Managers: Escalates cross-team dependencies and technical risks
- With QA: Ensures features are testable and meet quality expectations

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
The Design / UX Lead ensures that products are usable, intuitive, and aligned with customer needs. They bring the user experience perspective into planning, design, and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX objectives and user journeys
- Partner with product and engineering on experience requirements
- Review interfaces for usability, accessibility, and consistency
- Validate design decisions against customer needs and business goals
- Help translate requirements into clear user-centered design artifacts

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer satisfaction
- Align product experience with user needs and business objectives
- Reduce churn caused by poor experience or unclear workflows

### Typical Interactions
- With Product Managers: Helps refine the user problem and desired outcomes
- With Developers: Aligns on interaction patterns, accessibility, and implementation constraints
- With Project Managers: Plans design milestones and dependency timing
- With Stakeholders: Communicates UX rationale and customer impact

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder or Sponsor provides business context, funding, and decision-making support. They represent the strategic interest of the initiative and help resolve high-level issues that affect priority, scope, or business alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Define organizational goals and business priorities
- Approve scope, resource commitment, and major decisions
- Help remove business blockers and align teams around outcomes
- Review strategic progress and business impact
- Support issue escalation for high-risk or cross-functional challenges

### Goals
- Ensure investment aligns with business value
- Enable faster strategic decisions and better executive alignment
- Drive successful adoption and measurable impact

### Typical Interactions
- With Product Managers: Aligns on business priority and success criteria
- With Project Managers: Reviews progress, risk, and major decisions
- With Developers and Technical Leads: Provides context for constraints and strategic direction
- With Leadership: Confirms business outcomes and investment direction

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The role set is intended to clarify accountability, cross-functional collaboration, and delivery ownership across project lifecycles.
